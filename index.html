📖 Grammar Rules

We use the Present Continuous for actions happening now or around now.

✅ (+) Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing
❌ (−) Subject + am/is/are + not + verb-ing
❓ (?) Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing?
She is reading a book now.
They are not playing football. / They aren't playing football.
Are you listening to me?

⚠️ Spelling rules:

  • Most verbs: add -ing → play → playing
  • Verbs ending in -e: drop the e → write → writing
  • Short verbs (CVC): double the last letter → run → running, sit → sitting
